## Major gaps to fix

The current Advance flow does not effectively filter the complete returned Event Registry/Entity Map archive and Brave results for potentially adverse articles. Build a versioned script/catalog with **at least 500 distinct adverse strings** and scan the **title and URL path of every returned archive posting and Brave article**. Include terms and phrases such as `fraud`, `cyberattack`, `sanctioned`, `jailed`, `terror`, `data leak` and `security incident`, plus the other policy families below. A broad word such as `risk` may help flag a candidate in context, but cannot establish an adverse finding on its own. Record which terms matched each URL/title, and explicitly account for metadata exclusions. The goal is to find candidates within the completed source searches, not to claim coverage of articles the providers did not return.

Feed **every potentially adverse candidate** to Gemini through the Advance AI7 EDD review, in resumable batches. For each successfully reviewed article, require exactly one public **Subject Relationship** value: **Direct**, **Indirect**, or **No Association**. Direct means the incident or adverse event directly concerns the screened subject; it does not imply the subject caused it. Indirect includes employees, related entities, similar names and past relationships when direct involvement is not established. No Association means the subject appears only incidentally or in unrelated material, such as an advertisement. Keep event role, identity confidence and adverseness as separate decisions. If the article or model review fails, mark the relationship unassessed and the candidate unresolved; do not force one of the three values.

Gemini must screen the **entire potentially adverse candidate set** before report selection. Rank reviewed adverse articles by report priority, then place **at most 20 distinct, highest-priority articles** in the Advance PDF. Keep all candidate decisions, source counts and omitted-from-PDF counts in the durable evidence register and export. The 20-article limit must never limit screening or suppress an Unsafe finding.

Use the Signzy security-incident/data-leak title and URL as a release-blocking recall test: they must enter the candidate register and reach article review. The final relationship, adverse classification and profile must follow verified article facts and subject identity; this example is not a finding that Signzy committed misconduct.

## Required behavior

1. Screen the **title and URL path** of every article returned by the configured Brave searches and every matching posting in the verified Azure Entity Map snapshot. Search+ publisher searches remain additive and built in for Advance. This is the returned source universe; do not claim to have searched every article on the web.
2. Use a reviewed, versioned lexicon of **at least 500 distinct adverse strings** across financial crime, sanctions, cyber/privacy, enforcement, workplace, safety, environment, insolvency and other policy families. Include meaningful inflections, breach/security-incident phrases and supported non-English terms. Normalize case, Unicode, punctuation and URL encoding; match token boundaries. Record matched term IDs, category and lexicon version. A hit is a **candidate**, not an adverse conclusion. A weak word such as `risk` must not establish an adverse finding by itself.
3. Keep the existing subject-name/alias/transliteration gate for archive title/URL extraction. For Brave, retain relevant subject hits before the legacy adverse-term filter. Merge and canonicalize archive and Brave URL/title metadata, retaining both source labels, publisher, date and search-call provenance. Deduplicate query overlaps and syndicated URLs where supportable. Mark metadata exclusions separately; do not silently count them as article-level clearance.
4. Send **every potentially adverse candidate returned within the completed source search** to the Advance AI7 EDD review, in resumable batches. Obtain article text through the approved Event Registry resolver for selected canonical archive and Brave URLs it covers, reusing one extraction on overlap; use another approved article-text path only if provider rights allow it. Keep the existing provider guards and source quote validation. If a text source fails, record the candidate as unresolved. A provider, model, cost or processing limit cannot produce `Safe` or quietly remove an article.
5. For each successfully reviewed candidate, AI7 EDD must return exactly one public `subject_relationship`: `direct`, `indirect`, or `no_association`, separate from adverseness and event/incident role. `Direct` means the incident or conduct directly concerns the screened subject; it does **not** by itself imply culpability (a company affected by a cyberattack can be directly involved as a victim). `Indirect` covers employees, former relationships, related entities and possible namesake/similarity matches. `No Association` covers an unrelated or incidental ad/footer/name mention. Keep a separate internal role and identity-confidence reason for policy decisions. If evidence is unavailable, leave the relationship **unassessed** with a review status; never invent one of the three values.
6. The model classifies adverseness, relationship, severity, legal stage, event policy IDs and an anchored quote. The server verifies the quote, subject identity and source attribution before a direct adverse proposal can become `Unsafe`. `Indirect` or uncertain identity alone is not direct adverse. Keep `Safe`, `Unsafe`, `Inconclusive` profile rules and the existing `all`/selected policy controls.
7. AI7 EDD assigns a report priority to each reviewed candidate. After classification of the whole candidate set, select **at most 20 distinct, highest-priority adverse articles** for the PDF. Order confirmed direct adverse first, then unresolved high-concern candidates if the profile is Inconclusive. Never choose the first 20 by search order. Keep full counts and the full durable evidence register for audit/export; a 20-item PDF is a display limit, not a screening limit. The JSON, PDF, case record and callback must agree on profile and counts.

### Completion invariants

- Every completed Brave result and every scanned V2 posting has a durable metadata decision. Counts reconcile by source and by canonical URL: selected, metadata-excluded, duplicate, invalid and source-incomplete are explicit.
- Every selected unique candidate has exactly one terminal reviewed decision or an explicit pending/unresolved state. Missing body, missing snippet, model failure, ambiguous identity, provider failure, budget exhaustion and truncation never count as reviewed non-adverse and never yield `Safe`.
- Every successfully reviewed article has exactly one public relationship value. A direct relationship alone does not establish culpability or `Unsafe`; an unassessed article has no invented relationship.
- PDF selection occurs after full candidate adjudication. It contains no more than 20 distinct entries; profile, total counts and full export remain independent of that display cap. JSON, PDF, case record and callback reference the same immutable assessment version.

Run a staged rollback by disabling only the Advance route/flag while retaining prior evidence.

The current per-screening cost guard is not an authorization for unlimited extraction/model calls. If the requested all-candidate review exceeds its approved ceiling, keep the checkpointed job in `budget_blocked` with an explicit Inconclusive assessment and pending count until a separately approved budget or narrower, recall-tested candidate policy is available. Do not deliver a final Safe callback or silently reclassify skipped articles as safe.
