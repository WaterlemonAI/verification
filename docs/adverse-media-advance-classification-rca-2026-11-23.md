# Adverse Media Advance classification RCA — 23 November 2026

Scope: Signzy isolated candidate runs and the current Advance branch at `289458de3668048a63da846ce15d58548d7b54db`. This is a read-only evaluation of classification behavior. It is not an assertion that Signzy committed misconduct. The current customer route remains off. See the [release gate readout](./adverse-media-advance-gate-readout-2026-11-22.md) for deployment and V2 status.

## Outcome and causal chain

The initial live Brave result contained the headline `India's Digital Identity Titan Faces Cybersecurity Scare, Highlighting Concerns With Digital ID`, but lexicon `2026-11-22.1` missed its title/URL. Lexicon `2026-11-22.2` added `cybersecurity scare` and selected the headline. In the later `ceaaecf` Signzy run it was extracted, submitted to Gemini, and included as an unresolved PDF lead. Gemini proposed `victim` / `direct` / `not_adverse` / `confirmed`, but its 202-character supporting quote was not a contiguous span of the supplied article text, even after whitespace normalization. The source verifier rejected the quote, so the public relationship stayed `null` and the candidate remained unassessed. The `f243758` prompt revision has not been exercised against the live provider: its isolated preflight stopped before provider calls when V2 fell behind V1.

The `ceaaecf` run returned 29 Brave results, 27 unique URLs, 16 selected candidates, and 11 metadata exclusions. Fourteen selected candidates had verified article bodies and two had only search excerpts. The retained run summary showed one reviewed candidate, 11 unassessed, and four unresolved. Finding reasons were 11 `article_evidence_unverified`, four `non_adverse_evidence_unverified`, and one without a failure reason. These are outcome counts, not proof that all 11 failures had the same underlying quote defect. The raw per-article `ceaaecf` provider exchange and article bodies were kept in the candidate's temporary `/tmp` and are no longer available; exact attribution within those 11 and four cannot be reconstructed from the retained summary.

## Mechanism in code

1. `lib/adverse-media-advance-evidence.mjs` selects title/URL matches and marks excerpts as `search_excerpt`.
2. `lib/gdelt-article-evidence-provider.mjs` accepts an article body only after body length, canonical URL, title similarity, and publisher/syndication checks. The finalizer invokes it in `server.mjs`.
3. `lib/adverse-media-advance-classifier.mjs` sends the extracted body or excerpt to Gemini, then independently computes the subject identity match and persists the batch result. It publishes a relationship only when `source_quote_verified` is true.
4. `lib/adverse-media-advance-policy.mjs` verifies the quote, identity, role, and non-adverse conditions. The profile stays `Inconclusive` when these checks leave selected candidates unresolved.
5. `lib/gdelt-summary-v2.mjs` and `scripts/adverse-async-candidate-qa.mjs` stop the new live run before classification while V2 is behind V1.

## Corrective work to validate

1. Make quote evidence deterministic: have the model select a bounded source span or return offsets into the exact supplied text, verify the span server-side, and allow one budgeted, versioned repair of an invalid span. Keep the independent quote gate.
2. Separate **subject identity** from **association with the incident**. Revisit the rule for a short trade name corroborated by a verified article body and independent identifiers or sources; add explicit Signzy-shaped tests. Accept a confirmed subject mention with `context_only` / `no_association` when the incident is unrelated.
3. Persist per-candidate diagnostic fields without raw body retention: extraction failure code, evidence grade, model schema outcome, quote length, quote match/offset, server identity reason, model identity/role, and final policy reason. Distinguish `model_response_complete` from `evidence_review_complete`.
4. Restore continuous V2 maintenance and parity under separately authorized production scope. Then re-run the digest-pinned candidate and 10 + 10 cohort, including direct victim, incidental ad, exact and paraphrased quotes, missing bodies, replay, and cost ceilings. Do not use an unresolved article to claim `Safe` or `Unsafe`.
