## Acceptance evidence and limits

- Synthetic and mixed-layout fixtures exercise multiple banks, accounts, periods, ownership states, products, and currencies. Combined native ADCB pages are separated and their page evidence points to the original PDF pages.
- Exact row and closing-balance reconciliation is tested. A mismatched balance remains unreconciled. Duplicate pages and overlapping rows are reported, and retained transactions are counted once.
- Joint-account credits are not attributed to an applicant. Account relationships show the evidence level. Paired self-transfers are excluded from income and expense aggregates.
- Facilities are separate from deposit liquidity and income. Circularity and irregularity outputs are review signals and contain no fraud or credit-decision conclusion.
- Authenticated admin and customer routes isolate jobs and exports. New raw and derived payloads are encrypted locally. Earlier local demo jobs may still be plaintext and are outside the new format.
- The scanner is optional only for the local demo. Production waits for the private ClamAV sidecar and fails closed when it is unavailable. A clamd error or positive detection fails the job before extraction; successful scans produce `quality.malwareGate: passed`.
- The worker compares OCR-read rendered pages with native PDF text where enough tokens are available. Disagreement routes affected pages to the managed Layout path, when approved, or the private OCR fallback. The result is marked partial with a review reason. The comparison is heuristic and requires further adversarial validation.
- The Azure Layout v4 adapter submits only base64 document bytes to the configured endpoint, validates the poll URL, translates words/lines/tables into the neutral contract, and requests remote result deletion. Local fixture tests cover this flow without network calls. Production is approved only for UAE North S0, with an application-level 100-page and USD 1.00 ceiling; the local demo does not send statements to Azure unless every managed-extraction gate is explicitly configured.
- PaddleOCR loads the pinned English and Arabic PP-OCRv5 ONNX models locally. A one-page scan produced OCR tokens; full PaddleOCR ledger recovery across representative bank statements is still unvalidated.
- A synthetic Arabic table with Arabic-Indic digits and RTL physical column order reconciles at the neutral parser boundary. The pinned Arabic OCR model loads and recognizes a rendered Arabic header, but a full synthetic Arabic scan remains unrecognized; this is an open accuracy gap. PDFs without interpretable table structure, unreadable rows, unknown ownership, and incomplete evidence remain partial or failed. Generic parsing is format-driven and has no bank allowlist, but it is not universal. The API always sets `decisionEligible: false` in this local demo.

## Owner/compliance decisions held open

The handoff explicitly requires approval for retention and deletion, legal basis and consent, managed extraction region, request/page cost ceiling, public upload/page limits, callback policy, PDF report release policy, FX consolidation, transaction taxonomy, lender policy and AECB boundary, manual review and appeal ownership, confidence thresholds, and production legal hold. None is inferred from the local implementation.

## Local verification boundary

The module appears under **Analysis** in `/admin`, alongside Forms. Uploads are available only when `BSA_LOCAL_ENABLED=1` outside production; otherwise the module shows a setup message. The standalone `/bank-statement-analysis` browser page has been removed. The gated API contract remains at `/bank-statement-analysis/openapi.json`, and the live `/openapi.json` does not advertise BSA. There has been no deployment, merge, Azure resource mutation, or production API activation for this work.
